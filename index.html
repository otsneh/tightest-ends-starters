How a starter is defined

  • A team's official starters are the union of ESPN's depth-chart TE1 and the first TE listed in each TE row of the club's own depth chart.
  • Co-starters count: if the club chart has more than one TE row (12-personnel), or lists TEs as “A OR B”, each of those players is a starter.
  • OurLads is only used (marked †) when a club page has no TE rows.
  • Inherit-zero: if every TE listed ahead of someone is ruled out (Out/IR) on a chart, that player is treated as a starter too (inherit-0).
  • Rostered starters must be dropped before kickoff; otherwise they get a manual 0.
